Retrieved 2026-10-08. This is research, not tax or legal advice.

## Vagyonadó (wealth tax)

**Status: draft bill, not law.** The Ministry of Finance (Pénzügyminisztérium) published the bill for public consultation on 2026-10-06. Comments are due by 2026-10-14. Business groups (VOSZ, MKIK) have asked for business assets to be excluded and for lower rates, so the text may change.

**Confidence: medium-low.** The container's network policy blocked kormany.hu and every Hungarian news site, so the bill text was not read. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of tax advisers' and newspapers' write-ups. Read the bill before relying on any of it.

### Valuation rules (as summarized)

Set values, not market values. A value computed by the statutory method can't be penalized later even if the market price differs.

| Asset | Method |
|:------|:-------|
| Hungarian real estate | Bought within the last 12 months: the purchase price. Bought 1 to 10 years ago: the purchase price indexed by the MNB house price index. Otherwise: the NAV valuation model, then an appraiser as the last resort |
| Listed shares, and shares regularly traded off-exchange | Closing price on the last trading day of the year |
| Unlisted company shares | (equity + 2 x earning value) / 3 x ownership share (draft annex 1). Earning value = average after-tax profit of the last 3 closed years / 15%, zero if negative. Equity from the last closed year; above HUF 500m add audited hidden reserves in real estate, participations, and securities. Holding companies (participations at least 90% of assets): equity only. Minority discount: 25% for 33% to 50%, 30% below 33%. Worked examples: [Privátbankár](https://privatbankar.hu/cikkek/penzugyi_szektor/erre-szamitson-akit-vagyonado-terhel-majd.html), [Békés Partners](https://www.bekespartners.com/publications/vagyonado-nem-az-adokulcs-hanem-az-ertekelesi-szabaly-dont/) |
| Crypto and investments | Market price on the valuation date |
| Foreign-currency assets | Conversion rule not found in summaries |

### Open questions

Resolved from summaries on 2026-10-08 (still to confirm against the bill text):

- Band edge: 1.5% on the base above 100bn, as modeled.
- Trusts and foundations: same threshold, shared across linked structures.
- Non-resident scope: by asset type, as above.
- Unlisted-company formula: as above, matching two published worked examples.

Still open:

1. Can non-residents deduct debts, and which ones? (The engine deducts only debts secured on an included asset.)
2. The FX rate for foreign assets (MNB rate on the valuation date is likely, not confirmed).
3. Rounding of the tax amount.
4. Whether the final act keeps 2026-12-31 as the first valuation date.
5. Minority discount edges: does exactly 33% get 25% or 30%? (The engine gives 25%.)
6. How hidden reserves are measured, and what "related settlors" covers for linked trusts.

## SZJA (personal income tax) 2026

Flat 15% (Szja tv. 8. § (1)). From 2027 the government plans a tax credit that cuts the effective rate below the median wage, down to 9% at the minimum wage ([proab](https://proab.hu/proab-news/karman-andras-bejelentette-jon-a-szja-csokkentes-de-csak-2027-tol/), [hvg360](https://hvg.hu/360/20260930_bertargyalas-vkf-minimalber-garantalt-berminimum-mennyi-lesz-2027-szja-csokkentes)). The 2027 credit is not enacted and not modeled.
